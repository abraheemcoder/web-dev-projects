Great practice idea. Since you're currently mastering **Stage 2 — HTML**, you should focus on **semantic HTML**, document structure, accessibility, and content organization—not CSS yet.

Below are **10 progressively harder "About Me" templates**. Use **only HTML**. You can style them later when you learn CSS.

---

# Template 1 — Beginner

**Practice**

- Heading
- Paragraph
- Image
- Horizontal line

```text
----------------------------------
|            My Photo            |
----------------------------------

About Me

Hello! My name is __________.
I am learning Web Development.

----------------------------------

Thank you for visiting.
```

- ✅ Profile picture
